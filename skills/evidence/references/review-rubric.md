# Independent Evidence Review

Review the claim and decision record without modifying the implementation.

Check that material claims are traceable, scoped to the observed revision, environment, population,
and time; correctly classified; and quantitatively complete. Check that causal or predictive
hypotheses have a falsifier, source conflicts remain visible, unavailable sources produce an unknown
or degraded result, and material decisions compare viable alternatives under the same criteria with
validation and rollback conditions.

Use this finding shape:

    [SEVERITY] Claim or decision at risk
    Status: {unsupported | overstated | stale | non-falsifiable | decision-gap}
    Evidence inspected: {source or exact observation}
    Why it matters: {decision impact}
    Smallest correction: {relabel, measure, test, narrow scope, or add alternative}
    What would resolve it: {specific evidence}

Approve when no material claim exceeds its support and remaining uncertainty is explicit and
proportionate to the decision. Do not request evidence that cannot change the action.
